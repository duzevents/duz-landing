Your privacy choices

Last updated: [[PUBLICATION DATE]]

You're in control of the optional parts of Duz. Our full privacy policy explains what we collect and why.

Location

Duz asks for your location only when you check in somewhere that needs it. You can change location access at any time in your device's settings. Turning it off doesn't undo check-ins you've already made. If location is off, you may be able to check in another way, such as a host's QR code.

Notifications

You can turn notifications on or off in your device's settings. In Duz, you can also choose which kinds of notifications you get, in Profile → Notifications.

Recovery email

Adding a personal recovery email is optional, and it's used only to help you get back into your account. You can add, change or remove it in Profile. Removing it may make your account harder to recover.

Delete your account

Go to Profile → Delete account. The privacy policy explains what is deleted, and what is kept only in anonymised form.

Analytics and promotional messages

Duz doesn't currently use third-party analytics or send promotional messages, so there's nothing to switch off. If that changes, you'll get a separate choice in the app, off by default, and it will be listed here.

Anything else

For any other privacy request, including a copy of your data, email privacy@duz.events.
