How a night works

  1. 1

    Join your way

    Go solo, bring a +1 or come with your group. Solo is a completely normal way to join — that's rather the point.

  2. 2

    Get your crew

    When the night starts you're placed in a small crew that mixes people from different colleges, and tries to keep you with the people you came with.

  3. 3

    Follow the night

    The app shows where to go next and what to do there. Check in when you arrive, then get back to the room — your phone can stay in your pocket.

  4. 4

    Play along

    Some stops have Quests: a pub quiz round, a challenge, something to find. Do them if you fancy it.

Friends can join from an invitation with just a name — no sign-up first. They get the whole night, not a cut-down version. If they're a Durham student, they can verify afterwards and claim what they earned.

Made for Durham

Durham is a collegiate university, and Duz is built around that: crews mix people across colleges, and the points you earn count towards your college's standing.

You sign in with your Durham university email, which is how we confirm you're a student. Guests you invite don't need one.

FAQ

Who can use Duz?

Durham University students, who sign in with their university email, and guests they invite to a specific night.

Do I have to go with friends?

No. Joining solo is completely normal, and you'll be placed in a crew when the night starts.

Is there a Duz app?

Duz works in your web browser on your phone.

What does it cost?

Each Duz shows its expected cost before you join.
